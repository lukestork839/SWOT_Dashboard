Figure 13. Absolute longitudinal water-surface profiles of the Kanektok River and Uyak Creek. Solid lines are the median water-surface elevation in 0.5 km distance bins across all open-water passes; shaded bands span the 5th–95th percentile of passes. Elevations are orthometric (EGM2008 geoid); distance runs downriver from the anchor point (0 km) past the bifurcation (dashed line) toward the Bering Sea. The metre-scale separation between the two channels is imperceptible at this vertical scale and is resolved in Figures 14 and 15. Data: SWOT water-surface elevations, May–October 2023–2026 (95 passes per river).
