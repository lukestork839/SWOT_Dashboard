Figure 15. Detrended relative elevation profiles of the Kanektok River and Uyak Creek: residuals from a single second-order polynomial fitted to both rivers pooled (the shared regional gradient), so the flat zero line represents that common baseline. Solid lines are median residuals in 0.5 km distance bins, shaded bands span the 25th–75th percentile across passes, and dashed horizontal lines mark each river's reach-wide median residual. Data: SWOT water-surface elevations, May–October 2023–2026, all open-water passes.
