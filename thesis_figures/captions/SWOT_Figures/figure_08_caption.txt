Figure 6. Reach-scale interval (local) hydraulic gradient profiles of the Kanektok River and Uyak Creek, plotted as absolute steepness (cm/km) against downriver distance from the anchor point. Slopes are the numerical derivative of the 100 m median water-surface-elevation bins after gap-honest Gaussian smoothing (σ = 2 km, an effective resolution of ~4.7 km full-width-at-half-maximum; bins separated by data gaps are not interpolated across), so the curves show the reach-scale envelope only; the backwater-scale structure, including the steepening near the bifurcation, is resolved in Figure 7. Data: SWOT water-surface elevations, May–October 2023–2026, all open-water passes pooled.
