Figure 5. Distribution of the reference hydraulic gradient across individual satellite passes. Each point is the reach-wide gradient of one full-coverage, open-water pass, fit with the Theil–Sen estimator on 1 km along-stream node medians; the bold line marks each river's median — the reference gradient used throughout this thesis — and the shaded band its interquartile range. Two early (2023) Uyak passes lie beyond the plotted range and are annotated on the figure. Data: SWOT water-surface elevations, May–October 2023–2026 (93 Kanektok River and 95 Uyak Creek passes).
