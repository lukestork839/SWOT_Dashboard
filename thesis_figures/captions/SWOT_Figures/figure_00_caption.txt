Figure 1. Regional setting of the study area: the coastal plain between the Ahklun Mountains and Kuskokwim Bay in southwestern Alaska (inset: location within Alaska). The Kanektok River — locally the Qanirtuuq — is drawn in red and Uyak Creek in blue, the river colours used throughout this thesis; the white square marks the village of Quinhagak (Yugtun: Kuinerraq) at the Kanektok mouth, and the white star marks the bifurcation where the two channels divide. Centerlines are from the field boat surveys; Uyak Creek is drawn at half the Kanektok's line width as a visual cue to the size contrast between the mainstem and the secondary distributary, which the map scale would otherwise hide (line widths are schematic and not proportional to channel width). Basemap: Esri World Imagery; the scale bar is corrected for the latitude distortion of the Web Mercator projection.
