Figure 2. Study area and spatial-normalization scheme (regional setting in Figure 1; inset: location within Alaska). Each channel's analysis extent is drawn as a lightly shaded polygon (Kanektok River in red, Uyak Creek in blue), with the contributing SWOT nodes overplotted at low opacity, so more heavily sampled reaches read as darker. The white circle is the anchor point (0 km), the common origin from which all along-stream distances in this thesis are measured; the white star is the bifurcation, and white triangles mark the fixed 15 km stage-reference location on each channel. Basemap: Esri World Imagery; the scale bar is corrected for the latitude distortion of the Web Mercator projection. Data: SWOT node positions, all analyzed passes.
