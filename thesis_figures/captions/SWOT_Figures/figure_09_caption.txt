Figure 7. Fine-scale (backwater-scale) interval slope profiles of the Kanektok River and Uyak Creek. The slope is computed within each pass — a robust sliding Theil–Sen fit over a 0.5 km window on 100 m water-surface-elevation bins — and then aggregated as the median across all open-water passes; shaded bands span the 25th–75th percentile of passes, bins imaged by fewer than three passes are omitted, and the final ~1 km at the mouth is trimmed to exclude the tidal signal. Panel (a) shows the full reach and panel (b) a zoom on the bifurcation; dashed horizontal lines mark each channel's reach-average reference gradient (Figure 5). Data: SWOT water-surface elevations, May–October 2023–2026.
