P8 — Figure 10's frame without the analysis (presentation figure, A/B partner)

Esri World Imagery at exactly the extent, figure size, zoom and furniture
placement of Figure 10, with everything derived from data removed: no analysis
polygons, no SWOT node clouds, no centerlines, no anchor, bifurcation or gauge
markers, no legend. Scale bar and north arrow only. Flipped back and forth
against Figure 10 in a deck, the two slides register exactly — the map does not
move, so the only thing that changes is the analysis overlay itself.

The Kanektok River reads immediately: a continuous teal braid with exposed
gravel bars, running from the lower left up to the bifurcation on the right.
The Uyak Creek corridor — the upper third of the frame — reads as pale, near
featureless floodplain crossed by relict channel scars, with the active thread
barely more than one image pixel wide at this extent.

That asymmetry is the point of the pair. In Figure 10 the two rivers are drawn
as analysis polygons of comparable width, filled with node clouds of comparable
density, and the Uyak therefore looks like the Kanektok's equal. It is not. The
polygon is fitted around the braid plain the SWOT pixels are drawn from, not
around the channel, and the node density reflects SWOT's water classification
over a wet braid surface rather than a single deep thread. The flip separates
what the analysis samples from what is actually in the water.

Two renders are provided. P8_fig10_extent carries Figure 10's degree ticks and
saves to an identical 1643 x 1152 canvas, which is what makes the flip steady;
use this one for the A/B. P8_fig10_extent_bare drops the ticks for a full-bleed
slide and consequently crops to a different box, so it will jump slightly if
paired directly with Figure 10.

Caveats carried over from P7: the Esri mosaic has an acquisition seam running
diagonally across the scene, with the northern (Uyak) half from a paler,
drier-looking pass, so some tonal contrast is the imagery rather than the
rivers; and a single scene is a single unknown stage. The width contrast is
qualitative context, not a measurement. See P7 for the close-up in which the
Uyak's thread is actually resolved.
