P3 — Superelevation, the plain visual (presentation figure)

The comparison with no notation: (a) a typical superelevated river, its bed
aggraded to floodplain grade and its water surface riding above the plain it
would spill into — the configuration that precedes an avulsion; (b) the
Kanektok River as measured (median geometry across the 64 DEM arcs), set
down into its own floodplain with the water surface below the surrounding
ground at every observed stage. Same true vertical scale in both panels;
the horizontal axis is compressed, so bank slopes appear far steeper than
the gentle grades seen on the ground. Panel (a) is a conceptual schematic;
panel (b) draws the measured median elevations (SWOT KaRIn water surface,
ArcticDEM digital surface model terrain, boat ADCP depth).
