P5 — The two channels, the plain visual (presentation figure)

Companion to P4 with no measurement labels: the Kanektok River and Uyak
Creek on the one floodplain they share, drawn as a composite cross-section
at matched distance from the anchor with the corridor between them
collapsed to an axis break (the ~2.6 km separation noted at the break). The only message is where each water surface sits: the Uyak
just below the floodplain, the Kanektok well below it — the Kanektok is
already the low road, so water leaving it would have to end up higher.

Though unlabeled, the geometry is the measured one (identical ground and
water levels to P4): median water surfaces from the 63-arc analysis
(SWOT KaRIn, median stage), terrain from the ArcticDEM digital surface
model, channel widths to relative scale. The Uyak bed is dashed because
its depth comes from the boat ADCP at the mouth reach only. Same true
vertical scale as P1–P4; the horizontal axis is compressed and the
inter-channel corridor omitted, so bank slopes appear far steeper than
the gentle grades seen on the ground.
