Figure 5. Absolute longitudinal water-surface profiles of the Kanektok River and Uyak Creek. Solid lines are the median water-surface elevation in 0.5 km distance bins, aggregated across all open-water SWOT overpasses (April–November, 2023–2026); shaded bands span the 5th–95th percentile of passes and represent the measurement spread at each location. Water-surface elevations are orthometric (referenced to the EGM2008 geoid); distance is measured downriver from the inland anchor point (0 km), just above the bifurcation (dashed line), toward the Bering Sea (~35 km). Both channels follow a near-identical concave-up longitudinal profile. The fine (~1–2 m) sub-elevation of the Kanektok relative to Uyak is imperceptible at this absolute vertical scale and is resolved in the elevation-difference and detrended profiles (Figures 6 and 7).
