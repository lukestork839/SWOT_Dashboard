Figure 4. The arc-based sampling frame of the terrain analysis. Both panels cover an identical extent, aligned pixel for pixel. (a) Esri World Imagery with the field-surveyed channel centerlines (Kanektok River in red, Uyak Creek in blue) and the two corridor polygons of the polygon-based elevation analysis (light fill, coloured edge). (b) The same extent rendered from the 10 m ArcticDEM digital surface model as shaded relief with a hypsometric tint (orthometric elevations; the colour ramp is truncated to its land portion so the near-sea-level coastal plain is not rendered in water-like blues). Thin grey lines are the iso-distance arcs along which all channel, bank-crest, and floodplain elevations are sampled — arcs of constant radial distance from the anchor point (white circle, 0 km), plotted every 2 km for legibility — and the dashed limbs bound the bearing sector sampled. Dark points mark where the DEM independently located each thalweg on each arc. The white star is the bifurcation; the scale bar in (a) is corrected for the latitude distortion of the Web Mercator projection, and the inset locates the study area within Alaska. Data: ArcticDEM v4.1 10 m mosaic (source acquisitions 2010–2021); centerlines surveyed by boat in 2025 (Uyak Creek) and 2026 (Kanektok River).
