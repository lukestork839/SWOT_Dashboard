Figure 8. Terrain slope of the valley corridor and its dependence on the length scale over which it is measured. Both panels share the reversed distance axis of Figure 11 (Bering Sea at left, anchor point at right, 3–34 km); slope is reported as downstream steepness (cm/km), computed by robust sliding Theil–Sen regression on the corridor median profile. (a) The pooled valley corridor differentiated at three window widths (0.5, 2, and 4 km); the dotted line is the analytic derivative of the quadratic fitted in Figure 11, and the shaded band marks the 3–6 km reach immediately below the bifurcation. (b) The Kanektok-minus-Uyak difference of the two corridors' slopes at the 2 km window, red where the Kanektok corridor is steeper and blue where the Uyak corridor is, with the interquartile range of the difference shaded. Data: ArcticDEM 10 m digital surface model, corridor-polygon medians.
