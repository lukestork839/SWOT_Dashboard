Figure 12. Superelevation of each channel's water surface relative to the floodplain corridor between the two channels. For each arc, superelevation is the channel water surface minus the floodplain reference (the median terrain of the corridor strictly between the channels); the dashed zero line is floodplain grade, so a channel poised to avulse would ride above it. Lines give superelevation at the median observed stage, and shaded bands carry it across the 10th–90th percentile of the stage range observed by SWOT. Profiles begin at 3.5 km, the closest arc at which the two floodplains are separable. Data: ArcticDEM digital surface model (floodplain terrain) and SWOT water-surface elevations (channel stage).
