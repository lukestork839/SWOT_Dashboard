Figure 9. Map of the superelevation ratio β = H_AR/H_M (Gearon et al., 2024) along the study corridor. Each painted stripe is one 0.5 km iso-distance arc of the sampling frame (Figure 4), clipped to the span between the two channels plus 400 m to each side and coloured by its Kanektok β class: β ≤ 0 (teal), 0 < β ≤ 0.25 (yellow), and 0.25 < β ≤ 0.5 (orange) — no measured arc exceeds 0.5. The 3.0 km arc has no defined crest and is left unpainted, and the field-surveyed centerlines ride on top in white so the channels remain locatable beneath the paint. Radius labels along the southern ends of the stripes give distance from the anchor point (white circle); the white star is the bifurcation, and the latitude-corrected scale bar and north arrow are as in Figure 4, over Esri World Imagery. Data: ArcticDEM digital surface model (crest and floodplain elevations) and the boat ADCP survey (Kanektok channel depth).
