Figure 11. The valley long profile and the channel water surfaces within it. All three panels share one reversed distance axis, running from the Bering Sea at the left to the anchor point (0 km) at the right and covering 3–34 km; the dashed line marks the bifurcation. (a) Absolute orthometric elevation: the grey line is the median of all 10 m ArcticDEM pixels inside the two corridor polygons, pooled and binned at 0.5 km, with its 25th–75th percentile spread; overlain in river colours are the per-arc water-surface elevations located inside each channel from the 2 m ArcticDEM. (b) The Kanektok-minus-Uyak difference of the two corridor medians, red where the Kanektok side is higher and blue where the Uyak side is higher, with a ±1 m reference band shaded. (c) The same difference computed between the channels themselves, on the identical ±3 m scale so the two panels can be read against one another. Data: ArcticDEM digital surface model (10 m mosaic for the corridors, 2 m strips for the channel arcs).
