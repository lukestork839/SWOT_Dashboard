Figure 10. The β record as distance profiles — the profile companion to the map of Figure 9. (a) Kanektok β against radius on the reversed distance axis, excursions above zero shaded warm and β ≤ 0 cool; the 34.5 km tidal-flats arc is clipped to the axis floor and annotated rather than allowed to set the scale. The inset shows the sensitivity of the median β to the crest-search window half-width. (b) The two ingredients of the ratio on each arc: H_M (crest − bed) and H_AR (crest − floodplain). β is computed for the Kanektok only, where surveyed channel depth is available. Data: ArcticDEM digital surface model and the boat ADCP channel-depth survey.
